🔁 Key Prediction Patterns

3-in-a-Row Flip RuleIf Big or Small appears 3 times in a row → next result FLIPS to opposite. Mean reversion is very strong in WinGo.
📈
Number Trend ContinuationIf numbers are rising (2→4→6→8) → next likely continues upward. If falling → continues down. Follow the trend.
🟣
Violet Alert (Numbers 0 & 5)Always produces Violet. If the last 3 results included 0 or 5, watch for another soon — they tend to cluster.
⚠️
Colour Reversion (6+ Rule)If the same colour appears 6 or more times in your 10 trends → very high chance the NEXT result is a different colour.
